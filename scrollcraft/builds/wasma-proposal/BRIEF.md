# WASMA home: tailored proposal

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** Asem wrote: "You can choose, just pick the best options you think that would work. I don't really care. I just want to see a high premium website that when customers visit it, they say, oh, this guide can actually make pretty websites and high value ones. So yeah, that's your job." He also asked: "You can also use Up-Upsplash for images. The website should have images and stuff in it."

Evidence: the launch plan at `/mnt/project-files/launch/first-clients-plan.md` (market Libya first; cafés, restaurants, dessert shops, salons; lead with the QR menu; prices to test; fix the "No ready-made bundles" line; WhatsApp is the main CTA with a prefilled message). Everything else below is an authored decision, labelled as such.

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe (authored).** Calm, exact, Mediterranean daylight, shop-owner practical. References: a well-printed restaurant menu card, a Tripoli old-city courtyard at noon, Monocle's travel guides.
2. **Journey (authored from the plan).** See it working on a phone, recognise my own week, scan it for real, see what it costs, see what else they make, see how it starts, send one message.
3. **Energy (authored).** Medium open, quiet second act, loudest third act, then steady and practical to the close.
4. **Feeling and the one moment.** See the curve. The moment: "I scanned the menu on their site and it opened on my own phone."
5. **What no other site does (authored).** The page is a proposal written to your kind of business. Pick café, salon, shop, guesthouse or property office, and every act regrades: the photograph, the headline, the demo in the phone, the highlighted package and price, the samples order, and the WhatsApp message.
6. **Distance from premium-minimal (authored).** Premium editorial with hard rules and square corners; photographic, not maximalist.
7. **One world or scenes (authored).** Distinct scenes. No continuous camera.
8. **Assets.** The WASMA mark (`src/components/brand/Mark.tsx`), the five working concept demos, and Unsplash photographs chosen through the Unsplash connector (credited in the footer, hotlinked per Unsplash guidelines). No generated imagery.

## Feeling curve

| Act | Feeling | Cause on screen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Hero | Recognition | Your kind of place, a phone running your kind of site, a table card with a real QR code |
| 2 Friction | Mild unease | Three plain lines from your own week, no image, lots of air (authored silence) |
| 3 Scan (peak) | Delight | The phone glides over the card, the camera locks on, the screen becomes the working demo; scanning with a real phone opens it |
| 4 Packages | Clarity | A priced ledger with your row inverted to ink |
| 5 Samples | Curiosity | Five working concepts pan past, yours first |
| 6 Process | Calm | Four steps, one message to launch |
| 7 Proposal | Resolve | A short document already filled in for you, one button to send it |

**Peak:** Act 3. "I scanned the menu on their site and it opened on my own phone."

**It's the site where** you pick what you run and the whole page turns into a proposal for you, down to the WhatsApp message.

**Authored silence:** Act 2 is deliberately still: text only, generous padding, nothing to operate. It is the quiet before the peak, not dead scroll.
