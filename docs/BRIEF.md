# WASMA next — creative direction

Authored from the user's existing brand requirements and request for a substantially improved new build. No additional interview answers or client facts are invented.

## Evidence and decisions

1. Vibe: the user requested premium creative studio, European/editorial, Swiss typography, subtle brutalism, experimental usability, polished and fun. Decision: a precise editorial exhibition of functioning digital products.
2. Journey: retain the requested navigation, hero, selected work, capabilities, statement, reasons, process, feature, services, contact and footer. Decision: organize these as opening → live work gallery → studio methods → immersive feature → practical services → correspondence.
3. Energy: the user wants scroll effects and polish. Decision: quiet opening, rising curiosity, a memorable interactive gallery peak, a calm methods interlude, then an immersive case-study moment and direct contact.
4. Feeling curve: recognition at the exact logo; curiosity at the opening promise; desire while trying three distinctive demos; clarity at capabilities; confidence at the method; immersion in the featured project; resolve at the brief form.
5. Signature: authored proposal, not a user quotation: the detached lime square becomes a viewfinder into real, live project interfaces. The visitor changes the preview by selecting a project; the square resolves back into the supplied brand mark. The reveal advances understanding of the work.
6. Aesthetic: editorial premium with restrained brutalism. Not a maximalist neon exhibition. Brand pages use 80–90% near-black and warm off-white, with 10–20% lime. Demo brands can have their own intentionally distinct palettes.
7. Structure: separate scenes and cuts, not an unbroken camera flight. No forced scroll hijacking. Mobile has its own composition.
8. Assets: supplied original logo collage; vector reconstruction from the previous build; original generated editorial assets in this starter; existing site and source as reference. The photographic assets are illustrations of fictional concepts, not real client evidence.

## Hero layer contract

| Plane | Content | Behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Far | Clean generated warm studio surface, without a subject baked into it | Very small displacement; optional quiet texture |
| Focal | Exact vector WASMA mark | Short restrained transform; independent of background |
| Near | Live project viewfinder/interface fragment built in HTML | Separate restrained translation; opens into the work gallery |
| Copy | Semantic bilingual headline and contact controls | Readable at the opening, midpoint and exit |

No generated photograph is a multi-layer scene by itself. Do not duplicate a photographic subject. Keep supporting planes physically and visually coherent. If the hero becomes too complicated, simplify the choreography before adding effects.

## Proposed scroll score

- Opening: immediate brand and value, a complete static first viewport.
- Transition: viewfinder reveal with a clear 1–1.5 viewport payoff; no prolonged pin before useful content.
- Work gallery: distinct interactive previews, useful project selection, optional brief sticky composition on desktop.
- Capabilities: normal editorial flow and meaningful disclosures.
- Statement: one directional typographic intervention with an accessible static equivalent.
- Methods: quiet typography, diagrams or ordered details; no repeated generic card reveals.
- Featured project: image-led immersive scene and a working demo entry.
- Services/contact: natural flow, validated enquiry, direct email and WhatsApp links.

Use at least four distinct device families where appropriate, never two identical ones consecutively. The number of effects is not a success metric.

Peak sentence: authored target, not a testimonial — “The little square in their logo let me look into and try completely different websites.”

Tell-someone sentence: “It's the studio site where the brand opens into functioning miniature worlds.”

## Success criteria

The visitor understands within five seconds that WASMA creates websites and wider digital products; can try meaningful examples; can navigate every scene with touch, keyboard and reduced motion; and can start a real project conversation without guessing where a button leads.

No arbitrary empty scroll is planned. Every deliberate pause must retain content and have a short, visible purpose. Validate actual opening/midpoint/exit frames rather than relying on code or build success.
